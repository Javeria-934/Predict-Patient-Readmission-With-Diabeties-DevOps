Clinical Accuracy Trade-Off Findings

  • Majority-Class Imbalance: In the test cohort, 88.84% of patients are not readmitted within 30 days. High nominal accuracy (~89%) can occur when an algorithm collapses to predicting negative for almost all patients, missing true readmissions.
  • Optimized Clinical Sensitivity: When tuned for clinical intervention, the pipeline identifies 1,358 true readmissions (59.8% sensitivity), dramatically outperforming uncalibrated baseline thresholds that only identified 25 to 39 readmissions.
  • Peak Discriminative Power: The primary gradient booster achieved an AUC-ROC of 0.6897, exceeding the published study benchmark of 0.6670.
